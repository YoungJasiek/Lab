Lab Developer Community: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

Welcome to the Lab Developer Community (the official open-source documentation portal and technical reference for the Lab Engine and Frozen-Life ecosystem, hosted at github.com/YoungJasiek/Lab). We take your digital privacy seriously and adhere strictly to principles of data minimization and transparency.

1. Scope and Controller

This policy applies to all visitors accessing the static HTML/JS documentation site, client-side tools, and community discussion mock interfaces. The project is maintained as an open-source technical initiative under the authorship of YoungJasiek.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Cookies)

We do not require user account creation, password registration, or payment information. We do NOT set or collect any tracking, profiling, or advertising cookies. Specifically:

  • Zero Tracking Cookies: The website does not store or process third-party tracking cookies or marketing identifiers.
  • Client-Side Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember functional user interface state: dark/light theme preference, sidebar collapse status, and offline discussion draft text in the Talk page.
  • Server Logs: Standard HTTP server request logs may be processed transiently by your hosting CDN or GitHub Pages for DDoS mitigation and infrastructure security.

This site links to external GitHub repositories, external contributors, and open-source packages (e.g., Lua 5.4, miniaudio, OpenGL references). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party sites.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, questions regarding documentation accuracy, or data requests, please open an issue on the official repository at github.com/YoungJasiek/Lab/issues.
