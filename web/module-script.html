CMake Integration & Compilation

To integrate LabScript.dll in your game or tool, include its headers and link to its import library:

target_include_directories(MyGame PRIVATE "${LAB_ENGINE_DIR}/include")
target_link_libraries(MyGame PRIVATE LabScript)
